# Label Hunt privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026. Label Hunt is made by Tiffin Games (tiffingames.com).

## The short version
Label Hunt does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no
purchases, and it does not use the internet.

## What stays on your phone
Your progress (levels solved, stars, coins, streak), your settings and your profile name and avatar are saved on your
phone, inside the app's own storage. Nothing leaves the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this save in your device backup, so a new
phone can restore your progress. That backup is made and kept by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us, and
you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Permissions
The app asks for one permission: vibration, for the short taps you feel when you play (you can turn them off in
Settings). It has no internet permission.

## Children
Label Hunt is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If a future version adds anything that collects data, such as ads or in-app purchases, we will update this policy
before that version is released, and say exactly what is collected, by whom and why.
