## Full description (4,000)
(Each paragraph and bullet below is one line, so it pastes without stray line breaks.)
Every jar has a label, but you can't read it yet.

Label Hunt is a calm word puzzle about sorting. Each board holds a handful of word cards and a row of glass jars. Four cards belong together in every jar, but the categories are hidden. Move the top card from jar to jar, drop it where you think it belongs, and work out what connects them.

A wrong drop bounces back, and it shows you a little more of the label: first how long the key word is, then its first letter. Stack the first pair of a group and the label moves on again. When four cards of one group share a jar, the lid screws on and the label types itself in.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
- Labels you earn. Every slip and every pair reveals part of a hidden category, like a game of hangman.
- Stars for clean sorts. Fewer slips, more stars. Three stars puts a gold lid on the jar.
- A pantry to fill. Every category you solve goes on your shelves, room by room.
- 510 levels in 17 rooms, from the Larder and the Kitchen to the Apothecary and the Grand Pantry, each with its own walls, wood and jars.
- New ideas keep arriving: herrings, abstract categories, wordplay groups, and frosted, labelled and corked jars.
- Every level is checked so it has exactly one fair answer.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- No timers and no energy. Think as long as you like.
- Undo is free, and an extra jar is free once a level.
- Stuck? A Hint points at a good move, and Complete seals a label you pick.
- A daily gift and a play streak for coming back, with savers for the days you miss.
- Plays offline.

Made for phones, in portrait, one thumb at a time, with Reduce motion, sound and vibration settings.
