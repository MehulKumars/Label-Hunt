# Economy simulation

30 levels, 10 a day. Prices: Hint 50, Complete 250, Continue 150. Ads: 12 a day at most.

| after level | room | free player coins | ad player coins | one-purchase coins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | The Kitchen | 579 | 1108 | 579 |

- free: wanted 13 Hints, got 13 (100%); player level 6 at the end.
- ads: wanted 13 Hints, got 13 (100%); player level 6 at the end.
- once: wanted 13 Hints, got 13 (100%); player level 6 at the end.

## Gates

- PASS free player can afford a Hint at the start of 100% of levels (gate: 33% or more)
- PASS ad player is never short of a Hint for more than 3 levels in a row (worst run 0)
- PASS free player's median balance is 441 (gate: 100 to 2,000)
