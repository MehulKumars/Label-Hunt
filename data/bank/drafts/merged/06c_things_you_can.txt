# SUMMARY
# New categories: 28. Tier 3: 27 (25 "Things you can X" + Things in a pod + Things in pairs). Tier 2: 1 (Things in a nest).
# Extensions: crack +3 (Nut, Mystery, Window), break +6, catch +7 (Train/Taxi/Tram/Ferry/Metro fix an undeclared
#   overlap with Ways to travel), draw +1, tie +1, body_parts +1 (Throat).
# Crossings declared between the verb categories (each word is listed in both or all):
#   Price (raise/set/pay), Time (set/save/tell), Page (flip/skip/turn), Table (set/book/clear), Kiss (blow/steal),
#   Flag (fold/raise), Card (flip/sign + draw), Record (flip/set + break), Deal (sign/strike), Scene (steal/set),
#   Glance (steal/cast), Doubt (raise/cast), Money (raise/save), Seat (book/save), Date (set/save + Fruit),
#   Bill and Debt (pay/settle), Question (raise/answer), Letter (sign/answer), Riddle (tell/answer), Truth (bend/tell),
#   Light (cast/shed), Name (sign/clear), Room (book/clear), Life (save/lead), Tour (book/lead), Screw (turn/drive),
#   Bargain (strike/drive), Rope (wind/skip + tie), Feather and Leaf (shed/nest), Straw (bend/nest + draw).
#   Cross-domain herrings: Raspberry (fruit), Thunder (weather), Gold/Oil (metals, energy), Butter/Honey/Jam (food),
#   Love (tennis), Heart (organs), Stomach (organs), Pound (units, currencies), Whale/Dolphin (sea), Coffee/Cocoa
#   (drinks), Hotel (phonetic alphabet), Wedge (golf), Dandelion (wildflowers), Cymbal/Gong (percussion).
# Deliberately avoided:
#   - Verbs that fit almost any object: lose, throw, pick, pull, read, pour, spill, change, hold, keep, climb.
#   - Brief suggestions dropped: peel (every fruit and vegetable peels: undeclared overlap everywhere),
#     pitch/toss/deal/shuffle/whistle/file (fewer than 8 instant members), plant (needs pairs with Trees,
#     Flowers, Vegetables, Herbs, Houseplants), spill/pour (any drink).
#   - Members equal to another new key: Cast (sign), Turn (skip). Germ (illness). Compliment (10 letters).
#     Difference (10 letters). Anchor (cast/raise both arguable). Horse (lead: any animal can be led).
#     Lunch (skip: kept Breakfast and Meal only). Hair (tie: arguable).
#   - Things in a hive/deck/stable: already covered by In a hive, Playing card terms, Words for a horse / Riding gear.
#   - In a fireplace: drafted, then cut ("fireplace" contains Fire, and Words after fire has Place).
# Key words that are also members elsewhere (the bank tolerates this, but the generator may want to avoid it):
#   fold (Ways to mix, Playing card terms), blow (Words before fish/hole), flip (Gymnastics moves), wind (Weather,
#   Two pronunciations), set (Words after sun/head/tea/hand), book (Things with spines/pages), skip (Ways to move),
#   strike (Ways to score), bend (Parts of a river), shed (In the garden), drive (Golf terms, Computer parts),
#   lead (Metals, Two pronunciations), steal (Basketball terms). Cast, nest and pod got PAIR lines.
# Unsure:
#   - Things in pairs: a classic, but "a pair of" covers body parts, footwear, goggles, maracas, stirrups;
#     it needs 16 PAIR lines to stay fair. Drop it if the generator struggles to place it.
#   - Things in a pod: exactly 8 words; "vanilla pod" is UK/EU (US says bean); coffee pod is recent.
#   - Things you can steal: idiom set (Show, Thunder, Limelight, Scene), but literally anything can be stolen.
#   - Things you can turn: a broad verb; paired with flip and Things that spin.
#   - Things you can settle: abstract; Argument, Dispute and Matter are close in meaning.
#   - Things you can drive: half vehicles (paired with Ways to travel and Boats), half idioms.
