# SUMMARY
# New categories: 42 (13 tier 1, 29 tier 2). Under the ~70 target on purpose: animals and nature overlap heavily,
#   and every extra habitat or animal grouping I tried leaked into existing lists (see "Cut" below).
# Extensions: 27 lines. 22 extend my own domains toward 12-16. 5 are outside my domains (Mythical creatures,
#   Instruments, Body parts, Footwear, Pizza toppings), added only to declare crossings my words create.
#   Birds is now 16. Chicken is added because notes.md lists it as a Birds crossing but the bank line lacks it.
#
# Crossings declared (word listed in every category it honestly fits), cross-domain herrings first:
#   Horn (animal parts / instruments), Heel (dog commands / body parts), Limb (tree / body parts),
#   Foot and Base (parts of a mountain / existing), Queen and Drone (hive / chess, titles, things that fly),
#   Truffle (fungi / sweets), Prune (gardening jobs / dried fruit), Water (gardening jobs / drinks),
#   Sow (female animals / gardening jobs), Red (seas / rainbow), Black (seas / words before board),
#   School (animal groups / town, words before house), Cherry and Walnut (wood / fruit, nuts),
#   Bamboo (wood / houseplants), Unicorn (horned / mythical), Gnome (garden / mythical), Mule (pack / footwear),
#   Anchovy (fish / pizza), Mouse (rodents / desk), Cricket (hop / sports, insects), Honey (hive / yellow),
#   Teal and Flamingo (water birds / blue, pink), Shell (coverings / beach), Skin (coverings / organs),
#   Wool (coverings / fabrics), Perch (birds do / fish), Tweet (birds do / animal sounds), Fly, Nest, Bench, Sundial.
#   Within animals: Frog, Toad, Kangaroo, Wallaby, Dingo, Camel, Vulture, Owl, Penguin, Seal, Walrus, Narwhal,
#   Beluga, Lemming, Squirrel, Chipmunk, Boar, Stag, Mare, Stallion, Pony, Foal, Fawn, Ram, Bull, Yak.
#
# Avoided on purpose:
#   Ox (2 letters). Koi (a fish; Fish is full). Dragonfly and Firefly (contain Fly, an Insects member).
#   Butterfly (same). Osprey (contains the key "prey"). Rosewood (contains the key "wood"). Seahorse and Starfish
#   (contain Sea/Fish labels). Woodlouse (UK; US says pill bug). Ladybird/Ladybug, Moult, Savannah, Plough,
#   Mould (US/UK or spelling). Coral (animal, rock or colour: pedantry). Orca, Bat, Koala-as-bear (pedantry).
#   Crayfish (near the key Shellfish; also freshwater). Taipan (Australian, would leak from Snakes).
#   Toucan, Macaw, Emu, Cockatoo, Magpie (birds, and Birds is full). Leech (pond life leak, gross). Pelt (skin of a
#   dead animal). Swing (also a dance and a music genre). Pond and River as members where labels use them
#   (River clashes with the Rivers label). Sea (clashes with Sea creatures). Root, Ring, Crown (clash with the
#   Things with roots/rings/crowns labels). Comb in the hive (honeycomb is a stretch). Lupine (lupin/lupine).
#   Cut categories: On a farm (every farm animal leaks into it), Animal products (every meat and dairy word
#   leaks), Black and white (Piano, Chess, Domino also fit), Seasons (Autumn/Fall), Natural disasters (taste),
#   Deserts and Mountains as proper nouns (Desert and Mountain are Landforms members; few famous names),
#   Seabirds (merged into Water birds), Parrots (clashes with the Parrot member), Marsupials (merged into
#   Australian animals), Evergreens (Palm and Olive are evergreen: pedantry), Riding gear (sport domain).
#
# Unsure (least sure first):
#   1. animal_adjectives: Simian, Porcine and Reptilian may be too learned for "widely known". Could be tier 3.
#   2. dog_commands: Down, Come and Drop are plain words; fair once the label shows, but weak as cards.
#   3. wildflowers: Bluebell, Primrose, Foxglove and Heather lean British. Clover, Daisy, Dandelion, Poppy are global.
#   4. seas_of_the_world: Red, Black and North work as "___ Sea" but feel lateral. Caspian is technically a lake.
#   5. creepy_crawlies: the label is a Commonwealth idiom (US: bugs). The members are global.
#   Also: Snakes clashes with the existing member Snake (Reptiles, Desert animals). Both are paired, but other
#   drafts (Chinese zodiac?) may use Snake. Ram is also computer memory, and North a compass point, if other
#   drafts add those. Colt is also a gun brand (kept: the horse sense comes first). Habitat groups (jungle,
#   desert, polar, forest) need many pair rules; the generator gets fewer combinations but no unfair ones.
#   Existing bank issue spotted: Trees and Things in a forest overlap (Oak, Pine are things in a forest).
#   I proposed PAIR: Trees | Things in a forest.
