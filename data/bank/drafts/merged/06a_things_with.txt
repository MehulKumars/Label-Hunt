# ---- Weak members of existing categories ----
# WEAK: things_with_rings Bell - "rings" is the verb; a bell rings but doesn't have rings
# WEAK: things_with_rings Phone - the same verb pun as Bell
# WEAK: things_with_tails Meteor - a meteor leaves a trail; tails belong to comets
# WEAK: things_with_roots Square - a square root is maths; a square doesn't have roots
# WEAK: things_with_crowns Hat - "the crown of a hat" is obscure, and players read a crown as a kind of hat
# WEAK: things_with_spines Echidna - not widely known worldwide
