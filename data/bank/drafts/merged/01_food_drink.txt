# SUMMARY
# Counts: 35 new categories: 12 tier 1, 23 tier 2 (32 food, 3 drink). 22 extensions to existing food/drink
#   categories, 3 cross-domain declaration lines, 44 PAIR rules.
# Short of the ~70 target on purpose. Most tier-1 food/drink sets already exist (Fruit, Vegetables, Meats, Breads, Sauces,
#   Breakfast foods...). The remaining candidates were open-ended ("In a salad", "In a sandwich", "At a restaurant",
#   "Snacks", "Things on toast") and would create undeclared overlaps with almost every food card, or were near-duplicates
#   (Berries gives itself away, Hot drinks = Drinks + Coffee + Tea, Flatbreads = Breads, Shellfish = Things with shells).
#
# Crossings declared (word: categories it now sits in; * = with existing bank categories):
#   Herrings into other domains: Kidney* (organs), Navy* (shades of blue), Lima* (capitals, phonetic), Palm* (trees,
#   body), Button* (sewing kit, pop), Dice*/Cube* (faces), Rib* (bones), Shoulder*/Knuckle* (joints), Skin* (organs),
#   Straw* (draw), Bottle* (necks), Cup*/Glass*/Mug* (on the table), Dash*/Black* (words before board), Red*/Green*
#   (rainbow), Poppy* (flowers, red), Rabbit* (pets), Cow*/Goat*/Sheep* (farm), Buffalo* (safari), Acorn* (forest),
#   Corn* (pop), Rice*/Cheese*/Cream*/Carrot*/Crab* (words before cake), Whip* (crack), Whisk* (kitchen tools),
#   Microwave* (appliances), Salsa* (dances), Swallow (birds, new), Bay (landforms, new), Sunflower (flowers, new),
#   Chestnut* (nuts, brown), Oyster (seafood, mushroom), Truffle* (sweets, mushroom, oils), Mustard* (yellow, sauces,
#   oils, seeds), Butter* (dairy, yellow, fly, baking, bean), Jasmine* (flowers, tea, rice), Canteen (drink from,
#   places to eat), Glug (ways to drink, recipe amounts), Clove* (spices, recipe amounts).
#   Within food/drink: Churro (street, sweet treats, desserts, Mexican, Spanish), Tortilla (breads, Mexican, Spanish),
#   Flan (desserts, Mexican, Spanish), Sushi (rice, rice dishes, Japanese), Miso (soups, Japanese, fermented),
#   Pickle (preserves, fermented), Chutney (sauces, preserves, Indian), Curd/Kefir/Ghee (dairy + preserves/fermented/
#   Indian), Black/White (tea, bean, rice), Coffee/Cocoa/Vanilla (bean + drinks/ice cream/baking), Soy (bean, milk),
#   Almond/Cashew/Walnut (nuts + milk/oils), Chocolate (brown, ice cream, baking, milk), Oats (grains, baking),
#   Toast (breakfast, ways to cook), Barbecue (meals, ways to cook), Buffet (meals, places to eat), Chop/Fillet (cuts of
#   meat, ways to cut), Slice (ways to cut, recipe amounts), Shake (drinks, fast food, ways to mix), Pepperoni/Sausage/
#   Chorizo/Salami (meats + pizza/breakfast/Spanish/Italian), cheeses in French/Italian/Spanish, breads in French/
#   Italian/Indian, Chai (tea, Indian), Matcha (tea, Japanese), Espresso (coffee, Italian), Teriyaki/Marinara/Guacamole/
#   Satay (sauces + cuisine/street food), Raspberry/Cherry/Hazelnut (fruit or nuts + ice cream), Waffle (desserts too).
#
# Words deliberately avoided:
#   Contested or regional: Tomato, Avocado, Coconut, Peanut, Strawberry (fruit/veg/nut disputes); Beet/Beetroot,
#   Rocket/Arugula, Prawn/Shrimp, Courgette, Swede, Mince as a noun, Skim/Skimmed, Caster/Icing sugar, Broad and Runner
#   bean, Canola/Rapeseed, Pit (US for Stone), Crumble, Cobbler, Ziti, Chuck.
#   Spelling variants: Dal/Dhal, Pita/Pitta, Pilaf/Pilau, Borscht/Borsch, Yogurt, Lasagna, Chili/Chilli, Kebab,
#   Souffle and Pate (accents).
#   Label-word clashes: Sweet (Sweet treats), Pizza (Pizza toppings), Breakfast (Breakfast foods), Seed (Seeds you
#   eat), Stew in a "Soups and stews" label (merged label dropped), Eatery (contains "eat"), Jellybean (contains bean),
#   Fruity/Nutty (contain Fruit/Nut).
#   Hidden second memberships left undeclared, so dropped: Gelato, Sundae, Sorbet (are ice cream), Wings (a cut of meat
#   and fast food), Edamame (a bean), Mochi, Kulfi, Cannoli, Biscotti (desserts or sweet treats), Lassi (drink and
#   Indian food), Fennel (vegetable and herb and spice), Garlic (vegetable or spice), Lavender (herb and flower),
#   Duck/Goose/Turkey (meat and bird), Leg/Thigh (cut and body part), Skirt (steak and clothing), Grate (kitchen tool),
#   Kinds of water (open "words before water" set).
#   Taste: all alcohol (Punch, Cider, Sangria, Chug), Ghost pepper, Tomahawk.
#
# Least sure:
#   1. Kinds of bean: 15 members with four colour words and three non-legumes (Coffee, Cocoa, Vanilla). Every one is
#      honest, but colour cards are bland. Risk words not dealt anywhere yet: Runner, String, Jelly, Broad.
#   2. Kinds of milk: Butter (buttermilk) and Banana (banana milk) are undeclared near-misses. Needs four label-collision
#      pairs (Drinks, Dairy, Baking, Grains).
#   3. Seafood: shares 5 of 10 words with Sea creatures. Kept as a food-domain cousin, paired away from Fish, Sea
#      creatures and Things with shells. Could be cut.
#   4. Seeds you eat: only 8 members; Hemp (cannabis association) and Flax are the weakest.
#   5. Tastes: no Sweet (clashes with Sweet treats); Umami may be tier-2 knowledge; Bitter has a mild beer echo.
#   Also watch: Preserves (8, Curd and Conserve weaker), Fermented foods (Cheese and Kombucha feel "technically" to some),
#   Kinds of mushroom (8; White/Wild/King/Honey are rare undeclared near-misses), Places to eat (Deli), Rice dishes
#   (Jollof less known outside Africa/UK), Trifle and Pavlova (better known in the Commonwealth).
#   Generator check suggested: a card word equal to another level label's key (Rice, Milk, Mushroom, Meat, Cake, Fish, Pizza)
#   is confusing even when not a member. I paired the cases I found; a tool-side check would catch the rest.
